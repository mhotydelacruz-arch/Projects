# PAYVERIFY
A payment verification, approval, and budget monitoring system for Filipino small businesses.

This proposed module is designed to help small businesses manage their payments in a simple and organized way. It allows business owners to review payment requests, check supplier and invoice details, and make sure there is enough budget before any payment is made. It also makes sure that payments are approved by the right person, and it keeps a record of every payment that has been completed.

With a centralized dashboard, business owners can see all of this information in one place. They can easily track their payments and watch for possible financial risks, such as duplicate invoices, unusual payment amounts, or suspicious requests.

The module also helps Filipino business owners tell the difference between legitimate and fraudulent suppliers and buyers. By checking details before paying, businesses can avoid scams and mistakes, which helps protect them from financial loss.



 # DISCLAIMER
  Due to the limited MVP development period and available resources, the PayVerify prototype was developed for testing and demonstration purposes only. It was tested using sample or authorized test data and does not represent the actual financial condition or performance of any real business. It does not execute bank transfers, automatically verify supplier information, or guarantee fraud prevention. Authorized personnel must still verify sensitive payment details before funds are released. Further development, security testing, and real-world pilot implementation are required before production use.
